Add `tooltip-danger-background`, `tooltip-danger-border`, `tooltip-danger-label` and `tooltip-danger-divider` for a tooltip that reports a denial or a failure, in both themes. The fill and border match `callout-danger-background` and `callout-danger-border`, the divider between stacked sections matches the border, and the text stays `tooltip-label`, so the tooltip keeps its contrast. `Style` and `StyleTailwind` get the matching keys.
